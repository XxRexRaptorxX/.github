## <img src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/minecraft_gamepedia/images/e/ea/Redstone_Bug.gif/revision/latest?cb=20201130224258" alt="curseforge launcher" title="I`m a bad bug!" width="40"/> Bug Reports

Found a bug, unexpected behavior, or experienced a crash?

Please report it in the **Issues** section of the corresponding [CurseForge project page](https://www.curseforge.com/members/xxrexraptorxx/projects).

### Before submitting a report:

- Check if the problem has already been reported.
- Make sure you are using a compatible Minecraft and mod version.
- Describe what happened and how to reproduce the problem.
- Include your `latest.log` whenever possible.

**Important:** 
- Please upload your logs to [mclo.gs](https://mclo.gs/) instead of posting them directly.
- For detailed instructions, read: [How to Report an Issue Correctly](https://github.com/XxRexRaptorxX/General/blob/main/how_to_report_an_issue_correctly.md)

> Everyone who finds and reports a bug in my mods receives the **Bughunter rank** on my Discord server!

## 🌍 Translations

Want to help translate one of my mods into your native language?

That's always appreciated! However, please contact me **before starting a translation**.

**Requirements:**

- Only translate into your native language.
- Good English knowledge is required.
- Make sure the language is not already available.

**Rewards:**

- Your name will be mentioned in the language file.
- You'll receive a special rank on my Discord server.

For detailed instructions, check out: [How to Translate My Mods](https://github.com/XxRexRaptorxX/General/blob/main/mod_translation.md)
